I currently work for the Wisconsin DNR Office of Applied Science as a Fisheries Research Scientist. We work in tandem with the WIDNR Bureau of Fisheries Management, federal, and tribal partners to investigate fisheries concerns across the state. I have worked on topics such as Walleye recruitment and egg quality, coarse woody habitat additions, and collaborative technical projects. I am currently working on a myriad of different topics, all related to alleviating threats to fish communities (and the human communities that rely on them).

Also, as of the spring of 2026, I am also pursuing a P.h.D at the Center for Limnology at UW-Madison. My research is focusing on a whole-lake assessment of a walleye spawning reef addition in Franklin Lake, WI, and identifying other potential bottlenecks that may limit walleye recruitment in this system. We hope to scale this project to the region at large and help fill in knowledge gaps for other struggling walleye populations. This project is a large collaborative effort in equal partnership among WI DNR- Fisheries Management, the Sokaogon Chippewa Community, the US Forest Service, the Great Lakes Fish and Wildlife Commission, UW-Madison, and the WIDNR-Office of Applied Science.

I would describe myself as a generalist jack-of-all trades scientist. I am interested in many different topics orbiting within the field of "fisheries ecology". I just enjoy digging into different applied problems, and continually challenging myself with learning new things.
